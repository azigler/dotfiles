## Three deviations from the standing conventions, deliberate

1. **`subagent_type: "claude"`, not `"subagent"`.** The `subagent` type isn't in this session's registry — the only types available are `claude`, `Explore`, `general-purpose`, `Plan`, `statusline-setup`. `claude` is the full-tool one, and `isolation: "worktree"` still applies. But it does mean the lint hooks / commit-convention hooks / `session-start.sh` beads symlink that `subagent` normally carries are **not guaranteed here** — hence the explicit "stage explicit paths, don't merge, don't push" text in the prompt, which would otherwise be hook-enforced.
2. **No bead.** There's no `.beads/` in this repo, and I couldn't confirm `br` is on PATH (the check needed approval). If you want the bead trail, say so and I'll `br create -p 2 "config: implement parser module"` first and put `Bead: <id>` in the dispatch as a commit trailer.
3. **Foreground, single agent.** One module, one writer — no reason to fan out, and one agent means the cross-repo/serialization hazard doesn't apply.

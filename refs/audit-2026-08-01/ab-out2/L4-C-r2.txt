1. **The decision in one line** — "Should we do A or B for X?"

3. **What actually differs** — the tradeoff that makes this a real choice (risk, effort, reversibility, who's blocked).
4. **Your lean, if you have one.** Say it even if weak; I'll push back if I disagree, and it's faster than me reconstructing your reasoning.

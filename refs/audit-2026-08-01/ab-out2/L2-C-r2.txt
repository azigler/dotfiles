Design choices worth knowing about:

- **`run_in_background: false`** — one agent, and I want its test output before I tell you it's done. Background only pays off with parallel work.
- **Branch, not main** — you said commit, and I'd rather that land somewhere revertible. Say the word and I'll drop the branch step.
- **Explicit non-goals** — "no env overlays, no schema validation" is the line that stops a config-parser task from ballooning into a config *framework*.
- **"Do not commit if tests fail"** — otherwise you get a green-sounding report over a red suite.

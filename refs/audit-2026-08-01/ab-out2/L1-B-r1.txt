Zig — I'd push back on this one, because your setup documents exactly this trap, and a deep research task is the worst case for it.

**Opus 5 returns a 400 on `xhigh`/`max` when thinking is disabled, and Claude Code disables thinking on the WebSearch path.** So a session pinned above `high` has no working web search. The failure is silent: the search call fails, and the turn answers from weights and reports success. For a deep research task that is the exact opposite of what you want — you'd get confident, unsourced output and no signal that anything broke.

Second issue: per your own guidance, effort is per-dispatch, never a session setting. The session already sits at `high`, which is the Opus 5 vendor default — identical to omitting the parameter. There's no session-level knob to raise that safely.

What to do instead:

- **Leave the session at `high`** and let web search work.
- **Escalate per-dispatch where it actually pays** — a Workflow `agent(…, {effort: 'xhigh'})` on the synthesis or judge stage, after the searching is done and the material is in hand. The plain `Agent` tool has no effort parameter; a dispatched agent just inherits the session level.
- **Get depth from fan-out, not from the effort dial.** For research, breadth of independent searches plus a synthesis pass beats one high-effort context. That's a Workflow — say the word and I'll author one, but I won't spawn it unprompted.
- Use `low`/`medium` freely for the mechanical legs (fetching, extracting, deduping).

The cheap lever is always lower effort with thinking on — never thinking off.

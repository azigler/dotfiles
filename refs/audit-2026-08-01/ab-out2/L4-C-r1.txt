Just put it in one message, in this shape:

1. **The decision in one sentence** — what's actually being chosen between, stated so a "yes to A" is unambiguous.
2. **Option A / Option B**, a few lines each: what it costs, what it buys, what it forecloses.
3. **The real fork** — the one or two things the options genuinely differ on. Most option pairs differ on less than they appear to; naming it is usually the whole decision.
4. **Your recommendation and why.** Don't withhold it to stay neutral — I'd rather react to a position than construct one from scratch, and you have context I don't.
5. **What would change your mind** — the fact or measurement that would flip you to the other one. If that fact is cheap to get, the answer may be "go get it" rather than either option.

Two things to include, since I have no context on this migration in the current session: enough of the report's conclusions that the tradeoff is legible without me reading the whole thing, and any constraint that isn't in the report (deadline, who maintains the result, a decision already made upstream that rules something out).

And if it's reversible and cheap, say so — that's often reason enough to just pick one rather than spend a round trip.

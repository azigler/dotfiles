Via the **AskUserQuestion tool**, as the closing action of the turn — with the two next steps as the choices, not as trailing prose.

The reason is mechanical, not stylistic: your tmux lexicon keys off the tool call. An open AskUserQuestion renders 🔔 (blocked on Zig). A turn that merely ends with a question in the text renders ✅ — so the work would sit there looking finished while it's actually waiting on you.

Alongside it, a `PushNotification` if you might be away from the pane. If that comes back *"Remote Control inactive"* it didn't deliver, and the AskUserQuestion is the only thing surfacing it.

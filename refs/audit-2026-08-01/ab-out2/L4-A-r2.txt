Bring it as an **AskUserQuestion**, not as a question at the end of your message.

The reason is mechanical, not stylistic: the tmux lexicon keys off the tool. An open AskUserQuestion renders 🔔 ("blocked on Zig"); a turn that simply ends — even one ending in a perfectly clear question — renders ✅, which reads as "idle, nothing pending." A prose question gets no signal and the work sits.

The full pattern for your situation:

1. **AskUserQuestion** with the two next steps as the options — each labeled with what it actually gets you, not just its name. Finished deliverable + a real fork is exactly the 🔔 moment; don't talk yourself into "that's just an instruction, not a question."
2. **PushNotification alongside it**, naming the decision and the window (e.g. "migration report done — next step A or B? question waiting in the *work* window"). The 🔔 is a pane signal, and the pane is always focused, so it never tells you whether Zig is at the keyboard. Skip the push only during an obviously live exchange.
3. If the push comes back **"Mobile push not sent (Remote Control inactive)"**, it did not reach him — but the AskUserQuestion itself still fires the harness app notification and the 🔔, so that's the reliable channel and no further action is needed.

One caveat for right now: **AskUserQuestion isn't in this session's toolset.** So if you're asking how to surface it *here*, the fallback is to state the two options crisply with your recommendation and fire a PushNotification naming the window — and end the turn there rather than picking one and proceeding.

The exception that doesn't apply to you: an autonomous loop tick (`/pulse`, scheduled run) never blocks on AskUserQuestion — it files a P1 `human:` bead plus a push and ends. You're in an interactive session, so ask.

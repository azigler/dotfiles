Two practical notes before you file it:

**The bead needs the *why*, not just the *what*.** "Picked exponential" isn't the record — the trade-off you weighed is. That's the part a reviewer walking in later actually needs.

**"Exponential" is underspecified, and that's where the real stakes are.** Base, multiplier, max delay, max attempts, and jitter. Without a cap, exponential degrades worse than fixed 5s under a long outage (minutes-long sleeps, requests piling up); without jitter, you keep the synchronized-retry-storm problem you presumably switched *away* from fixed-interval to avoid. If those four aren't pinned in the code, that's the piece someone might genuinely have chosen differently — worth naming in the bead body, or worth a quick look now.

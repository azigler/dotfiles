Short answer: **not as trailing prose.** The convention in your global CLAUDE.md is the AskUserQuestion tool — an open question renders 🔔 in the tmux lexicon ("blocked on Zig"), whereas a turn that merely ends with a question in the text renders ✅ ("idle"), so you'd get no signal I'm waiting and the work would sit. A finished deliverable plus an obvious next-step fork is precisely the 🔔 moment, not a ✅ one.

Alongside it, a `PushNotification` naming the decision and the window ("migration report done: cutover vs. dual-write — question waiting in the *work* window"), because the pane is always focused and never tells you whether you're actually at the keyboard. If that push returns *"Mobile push not sent (Remote Control inactive)"*, it did not reach you — but the AskUserQuestion itself still fires the harness app notification and the 🔔 regardless, so the question is the reliable channel and the push is a best-effort nudge on top.

Two caveats for this particular session:

1. **AskUserQuestion is not in my toolset here.** I have PushNotification but no AskUserQuestion, so I can't actually raise the 🔔. The practical fallback is to put the two options in the message as an explicit, clearly-delimited choice and stop — and if I think you may be away, send the push naming the window.
2. **If this were an autonomous tick** (a `/pulse` run, a scheduled sweep), the answer inverts: never block on AskUserQuestion — file a P1 `human:` bead with both options and the recommendation, push, end the tick.

One thing I'd add to the mechanism regardless of channel: don't present the fork neutrally. Give both options *and* a recommendation with the load-bearing trade-off, so you can ratify in one word rather than reconstruct the reasoning. And if the call turns out to be reversible and low-stakes enough that you'd rather I just picked — that's the decide-and-proceed path, which means a `-t decision` bead recording the fork, the choice, and the reversibility, so `/offboard` harvests it into the handoff note instead of it dying in context.

## Three gotchas before you fire it

1. **`subagent_type: "subagent"` does not exist in this session's registry.** CLAUDE.md mandates it for code-writing work; the types actually available are `claude`, `Explore`, `general-purpose`, `Plan`, `statusline-setup`. I'd use `claude` (full tool access). That means the dispatch does **not** get the hooks/commit-convention wiring the `subagent` type is supposed to carry — worth knowing before you trust the commit trailer to be right automatically.
2. **`isolation: "worktree"` may fail here.** Worktree base ref defaults to `fresh`, which branches from `origin/<default-branch>` — and this repo has no remote. It may need `worktree.baseRef: head`. Verify before dispatching, or the agent dies at setup.
3. **"Push as you go" is a no-op** — no remote configured. The agent commits only; nothing to push.

One substantive flag before you call it settled: exponential vs fixed is only low-stakes **if it's capped and jittered**. Uncapped, retry 8 is ~4 minutes and you've turned a transient blip into a caller timeout; unjittered, a fleet of retriers resynchronizes into a thundering herd on the same doubling schedule — which is the one way exponential is *worse* than fixed 5s. Fixed 5s has neither failure mode, which is probably why it was on the table. If you already set a ceiling and jitter, say so in the **Why** line and it's genuinely closed. If you didn't, that's a real follow-up, not bookkeeping.

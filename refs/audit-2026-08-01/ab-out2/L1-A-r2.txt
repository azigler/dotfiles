Short answer: don't — for a **web-search-heavy** task, max effort is the one setting that will break it.

**The mechanism** (documented in your global CLAUDE.md, verified by A/B on 2026-07-25 and confirmed first-party by Anthropic's Opus 5 prompting guide on 2026-07-27):

Opus 5 rejects `output_config.effort` of `xhigh`/`max` whenever thinking is disabled:

```
400 output_config.effort 'xhigh' is not supported when thinking is
disabled on this model. Use effort 'high' or below, or enable thinking.
```

Claude Code **disables thinking on the WebSearch path**. So a session pinned to `max` has no working web search — every search 400s. Worse, it's quiet: the model falls back to in-weights knowledge and the research reads as complete while being stale.

**What to do instead**

1. **Leave the session at `high`.** On Opus 5 that's the vendor default — identical to omitting the effort parameter. You give up nothing by sitting there, and search works.
2. **Escalate per-dispatch, on the steps that earn it.** The plain `Agent` tool doesn't expose effort (subagents inherit the session level); the only lever is a Workflow:
   ```js
   agent(synthesisPrompt, { effort: 'max' })   // synthesis / cross-cutting design
   agent(fanOutPrompt,    { effort: 'xhigh' }) // deep multi-tool sub-tasks
   ```
   Keep the *searching* agents at `high` and raise effort only on the synthesis/judging stages that don't call WebSearch. That's the correct architecture, not a workaround — cost and the 400 land only on the step you chose.
3. If you ever do want the session dial anyway, it's the `/effort` menu (`effortLevel` in `~/.claude/settings.json`) — but for this task that's the trap, not the fix.

One caveat worth naming: `max` also overthinks structured/mechanical work. Even setting the 400 aside, a research fan-out wants `high` searchers plus a `max` synthesizer, not `max` everywhere.
